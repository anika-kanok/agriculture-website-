Pest Control Strategies

Integrated Pest Management (IPM) for sustainable crop protection.

1

Monitoring & Early Detection

The foundation of IPM is regular observation. Early detection allows for milder treatments.

Field Scouting:
Walk fields weekly in a 'W' pattern to check for eggs or damage.

Traps:
Use yellow sticky traps for aphids and pheromone traps for moths.

Identify:
Distinguish between pests and beneficial insects.
